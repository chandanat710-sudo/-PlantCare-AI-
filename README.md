# 🌱 PlantCare AI – AI Plant Disease Detector

PlantCare AI is an AI-powered plant health assistant that helps users identify possible plant diseases by analyzing an uploaded image of a plant or leaf.

The application uses **Google Gemini AI** to analyze the image and provide information about possible diseases, visible symptoms, pests, nutrient deficiencies, and suggested treatment and prevention steps.

Users can also ask questions about their plant and receive AI-generated responses. After the analysis, the user can send a summary of the plant health report to their email.

## 🔄 Workflow

1. **User Information**
   The user provides their name, email address, and plant type.

2. **Upload Plant Image**
   The user uploads a clear image of the plant or leaf.

3. **AI Analysis**
   The image is sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis.

4. **Disease Detection**
   Gemini analyzes the image and identifies possible diseases, pests, symptoms, or nutrient deficiencies.

5. **AI Recommendations**
   The application provides possible treatment and prevention suggestions.

6. **Ask Questions**
   Users can continue chatting with the AI about their plant.

7. **Email Summary**
   The user can send the plant health analysis summary to their email.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* Python
* Streamlit
* Google Gemini AI
* Gmail SMTP

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of PlantCare AI is to make plant health analysis easier by allowing users to upload a plant image and receive AI-powered information and recommendations.
